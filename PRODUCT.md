# Therzie Mods Wiki

## Platform
Static website on GitHub Pages. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Data comes from the in-game `wikidump` command (separate TherzieWikiDumper plugin).

## Users
- Players new to the mods, figuring out what's in them and how to use it (not how to install).
- Players mid-game looking something up: a recipe, how to tame a creature, how to summon a boss, comparing weapons and armor.

## Product Purpose
Fast reference for Therzie's Armory, Warfare, Monstrum and Wizardry: items, recipes and upgrades, build pieces, creatures (resistances, drops, spawns, taming) and locations/altars.

## Operating Context
Often opened on a second monitor or phone while playing. Users scan for one fact and leave.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Vanilla items appear by name only (no vanilla icons).
- Location pictures are hand-taken screenshots.
- No raids, no creature attacks, no custom minimap icons.

## Brand Commitments
None beyond "easy to read". Looking AI-made is fine.

## Product Principles
- Readability first: high contrast text, short line lengths, tabular numbers.
- Icons must be readable: Therzie's icons are mostly dark, so they always sit on a light well.
- Every page answers one lookup quickly; cross-links connect recipes, drops, taming and altars.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast minimum for all text including placeholders; visible focus rings; works at phone width without page-level horizontal scroll.
